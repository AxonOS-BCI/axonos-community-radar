How we know

Every fact says
how it is known.

Observed

Read from a primary source: a registry, a regulator, a filing, a paper, code, a policy.

Declared

Stated by the company itself, and marked as its word.

Inferred

A reading of observed facts — reviewed by a person before you see it, and never shown as fact.

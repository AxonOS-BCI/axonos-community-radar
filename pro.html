  • That every project a field watch surfaces is relevant to you.

Questions.

What exactly is in a Field Brief?

10 to 14 pages: the field in numbers; movers; new entrants, reviewed; builders and the teams behind them; the stack: standards and interoperability; risks: dormancy and dependencies; your watchlist. Every project in it is checked by an analyst, and every figure is cited. The sample shows four of its pages, built from real data.

Where do founder and company details come from?

From public sources: company registries, company sites, press, papers and Crunchbase. Each line carries its source and the date it was checked. Nothing private, and nothing that a person has not published about their own work.

How do I pay, and can I pay by card or in crypto?

By bank transfer against an invoice, in USD or EUR. There is no card checkout, and PRO is not sold for crypto. A voluntary Dogecoin contribution to the free map is on Support, and it buys no plan.

Do I need a GitHub account?

For the watch, yes: the digest arrives in a GitHub repository. The Field Brief arrives by email and needs nothing.

Can you watch repositories that are not on the public map?

Yes. Anything public on GitHub can go on your watchlist; it is scanned the same way whether or not it cleared the map's gate.

Who can see my watchlist and my brief?

The collaborators you add, and us, because we prepare them. They are never published and never feed the public map.

Is it scanned more often than the public map?

No. Your list is scanned with the public scan, every three hours. There is no separate faster schedule.

What access does Radar need to our GitHub?

None. Your repository is private and kept by us; we invite the accounts you name, read-only. Radar never asks for access to your own account or organisation. The access model.

How do we cancel?

Cancel by email to connect@axonos.org. Monthly plans end at the close of the month already paid.

What if the engine stops?

Its run record is public in this repository. If it misses a day of deliveries, your term is extended by that day and the gap is noted in your repository.

Prices, entitlements, guarantees and terms are set out in data/commercial.json, and the PRO page is tested against it on every change. The example digest is drawn from this site's weekly data when the page opens; the sample brief is dated 3 October 2026.

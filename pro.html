    Mail connect@axonos.org with the plan and the repositories you want watched.

  2. Get an order id

    We reply with one, such as AXR-2026-000001, and how to pay. It ties the payment to the plan and the term.

  3. Pay

    Send the payment, then reply with the transaction id. The chain is public, so either of us can check it.

  4. Read your first digest

    We set up your repository and invite you. The first digest arrives within seven days of the payment confirming.

What we promise. And what we cannot.

We promise

  • Your watchlist is scanned with every public scan, every three hours.
  • What changed is written to your private repository after each scan.
  • An issue is opened when something changed, and not when nothing did.
  • The first digest arrives within seven days of the payment confirming.

We cannot promise

  • That the public map, or any field watch, finds every BCI project that exists.
  • That a third-party repository is correct, maintained or fit for any use.
  • GitHub's own availability, or that a repository stays public, named or online.
  • That every project a field watch surfaces is relevant to you.

A payment never buys a place on the map.

It never buys a place on the public map. It never changes a score, a tier or a piece of evidence. And nothing on the public map is taken away to make a paid version of it. PRO watches what you choose; the map stays the same for everyone.

Questions.

Do I need a GitHub account?

Yes — the digest arrives in a GitHub repository. If that does not suit you, write to us before ordering.

Can you watch repositories that are not on the public map?

Yes. Anything public on GitHub can go on your watchlist; it is scanned the same way whether or not it cleared the map's gate.

Who can see my watchlist?

The collaborators you add, and us, because we generate it. It is never published and never feeds the public map.

Is it scanned more often than the public map?

No. Your list is scanned with the public scan, every three hours. What changes between plans is how much you watch and how often you are told.

How do I cancel?

Tell us before the next month starts. The monthly plans stop at the end of the month already paid; the launch year is prepaid for its twelve months.

What moved, in four lines

  1. The field is alive, and it is mostly acquisition.113 of 120 projects changed in the last month. 56 of 120 are hardware and acquisition; real-time and embedded is the thinnest layer, with one project.
  2. Interoperability is still the exception.30 of 120 projects declare a standard. MNE/FIF (10), BIDS (7) and LSL (7) lead; 101 links connect the projects that share one.
  3. Consumer neural input is drawing outside developers.Community controls for Meta's neural wristband gained 13 stars in a week around a 1 October release, with a single maintainer.
  4. The newcomers are research code.Of 6 new projects, 3 are in scope, 2 adjacent and 1 a concept. None is yet a product; two are worth a second look in a quarter.

The stack, and the risks in it

What the field is standardising on.

  • FIF/MNE10
  • BIDS7
  • LSL7
  • BrainFlow4
  • NWB4
  • EEGLAB2
  • FieldTrip2
  • OpenBCI2
  • EDF1

Read. The MNE file format and BIDS organise data; LSL and BrainFlow move it from devices to software, so a device that speaks both is usable by every project here that declares either. None of the declared standards covers timing or consent.

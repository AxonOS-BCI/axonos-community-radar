The plain terms

  • Contributions are voluntary. They are not purchases, investments, securities, governance rights, token allocations or promises of financial return.
  • No product entitlement is granted for voluntary contributions — everything here is already free for everyone. A Dogecoin tip is a tip; it is not a plan, and it grants nothing a plan grants.
  • Paid plans are a service. The monthly plans are billed monthly and cancellable at any time; the launch year is prepaid for twelve months from payment confirmation and is not a monthly subscription. They buy the scanning of a list you name and its delivery to a private repository. They do not gate anything already published, and the published data — including its complete history — stays free whether or not anyone subscribes.
  • The scanning engine is closed. This repository is the presentation layer and is open under its licence; axonos-radar-core, which performs the scan and the scoring, is private. Stated here rather than left to be discovered.
  • Crypto transactions are irreversible. Verify the complete address before sending. Network fees and market volatility are the sender's responsibility.
  • AxonOS will never ask for your wallet seed phrase, private key or remote wallet access.
  • Commercial licence payments (e.g. for the Neural Boundary Game's commercial licence) require a written agreement and invoice; fiat (SGD or USD) is the default. See CRYPTO_PAYMENT_TERMS.md or write to connect@axonos.org.

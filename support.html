The plain terms.

  • Contributions are voluntary. They are not purchases, investments, securities, governance rights, token allocations or promises of financial return.
  • No product entitlement comes with a contribution — everything here is already free for everyone. A Dogecoin tip is a tip; it is not a plan, and it grants nothing a plan grants.
  • Paid plans are a service. They are invoiced and paid by bank transfer: the monthly plans are invoiced monthly and cancellable at any time, and a Field Brief is invoiced once. They buy the scanning of a list you name, its delivery to a private repository, and briefs written by an analyst. They do not gate anything already published, and the published data — including its complete history — stays free whether or not anyone subscribes.
  • The scanning engine is closed. This repository is the presentation layer and is open under its licence; axonos-radar-core, which performs the scan and the scoring, is private. What is public and what is not.
  • Crypto transactions are irreversible. Verify the complete address before sending. Network fees and market volatility are the sender's responsibility.
  • AxonOS will never ask for your wallet seed phrase, private key or remote wallet access.
  • Commercial licence payments — for example for the Neural Boundary Game's commercial licence — require a written agreement and invoice; fiat (SGD or USD) is the default. Write to connect@axonos.org.

Contributions go to one public Dogecoin address, the same everywhere on this site and checked by the repository's tests on every change. Inclusion on the map is never for sale.

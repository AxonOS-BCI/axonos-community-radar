Support the AxonOS organism

The radar that maps the open BCI field every 3 hours, the Neural Boundary Game, and the open-source real-time neural OS behind them are built and kept free by one person. No ads, no tracking, no tokens. This site is open; the scanning engine behind it is not. The map you are looking at, every data file it writes, and the whole of its history are public and always will be. The scanner that produces them runs in a closed repository, and the paid plans below sell what that engine can do for you that the published files cannot.

Plans

What is already free is listed first, in full, including the parts a pricing page usually leaves out. The paid lines are the things the closed engine does on request, which the published files cannot do on their own.

Free, and not a tier

The live map rescanned every 3 hours, the stats, the weekly digest, the RSS feed, the ecosystem manifest, the health badge, and every data file the scan writes. No account, no key, no limit.

The full history is free too, and you do not need us to serve it. Every snapshot since 25 June 2026 is in this repository's git history — git log -p -- data/radar.json gives you all 2 811 of them. We are not going to charge for a file you can already clone.

What is not open: the scanning engine, axonos-radar-core, is a private repository. This site, its build and its data are open; the thing that produces the data is not. That is worth knowing before you decide what a subscription is worth to you.

The plain terms

  • Contributions are voluntary. They are not purchases, investments, securities, governance rights, token allocations or promises of financial return.
  • No product entitlement is granted for voluntary contributions — everything here is already free for everyone. A Dogecoin tip is a tip; it is not a plan, and it grants nothing a plan grants.
  • Paid plans are a service, billed monthly and cancellable at any time. They buy delivery, request quota and scanning of a list you name. They do not gate anything already published, and the published data — including its complete history — stays free whether or not anyone subscribes.
  • The scanning engine is closed. This repository is the presentation layer and is open under its licence; axonos-radar-core, which performs the scan and the scoring, is private. Stated here rather than left to be discovered.
  • Crypto transactions are irreversible. Verify the complete address before sending. Network fees and market volatility are the sender's responsibility.
  • AxonOS will never ask for your wallet seed phrase, private key or remote wallet access.
  • Commercial licence payments (e.g. for the Neural Boundary Game's commercial licence) require a written agreement and invoice; fiat (SGD or USD) is the default. See CRYPTO_PAYMENT_TERMS.md or write to connect@axonos.org.
